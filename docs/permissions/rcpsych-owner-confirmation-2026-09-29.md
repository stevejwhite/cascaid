# RCPsych content permission — owner confirmation

Recorded 29 September 2026 from the project owner's message:

> I confirm permission to use content from the rcpsych.ac.uk site

This records the owner's attestation of permission for the application's previously
requested syllabus and examination-information use. It is not an independently verified
publisher grant or an assertion that the website has an open-content licence. RCPsych
copyright and attribution are retained. No separate publisher correspondence, licence
identifier, expiry date or redistribution terms were supplied in this conversation.

The approval is applied to the existing bounded document list:

| Registry entry | Document scope |
|---|---|
| `mrcpsych_paper_a` | Paper A sections of the February 2021 syllabus |
| `mrcpsych_paper_b` | Paper B sections of that syllabus and the May 2011 Critical Review syllabus |
| `mrcpsych_casc` | March 2023 CASC syllabus and blueprint |
| `rcpsych` | Examination eligibility and regulations, document dated 27 May 2026 |

Exact publisher URLs and version/content hashes are recorded in each entry's
`approved_items` in `config/sources.yaml`, using the already identified documents in
`config/rcpsych-references.json`. The approved uses are local indexing, retrieval,
attributed educational display and syllabus-item extraction for PsychiatryEduAI.
The project does not treat this as permission to train model weights, redistribute
source dumps, crawl the whole site or access eLearning, question banks, station scripts,
videos or other separately restricted material.

Permission approval does not establish examination applicability or content accuracy.
The February 2021 written syllabus remains scoped to examinations through December
2026 in the reference manifest. The January 2027 replacement still needs identification
and version verification before it can be added to the approved item list.

The four decisions are recorded through the existing human-review journal, with the
owner identified as the source of the confirmation and Codex as its recorder. This
updates source approval settings but does not itself download or index the documents.
